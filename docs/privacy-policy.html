Manage Everything: Privacy Policy

Effective 29 October 2026

Manage Everything is an Android app for organising goals, plans, tasks, habits, a daily schedule, notes, books and quotes. This policy explains what happens to the information you put into it. In short: everything stays on your device. The app does not collect, send or share any of your data.

Information the app stores

Everything you enter (your items, links, tags, reminders, habit history and settings) is saved in the app's private storage on your device. It is not sent to the developer or to anyone else.

Information the app collects

None. The app has no user accounts, analytics, advertising, crash reporting or tracking of any kind. It does not request permission to use the internet, so it cannot send data anywhere.

Permissions

  • Notifications: to show the reminders you set.
  • Alarms and reminders (exact alarms): so reminders arrive at the time you chose.
  • Run at startup: to set your reminders again after your phone restarts or the app is updated.

None of these permissions give the app access to your personal information.

Exporting and importing

You can export your data to an Excel file, or import one, from the app's Settings. This only happens when you choose to, and the file goes to, or comes from, the place you pick with Android's file picker. What happens to an exported file after that, for example if you save it to a cloud drive, is up to you and the service you use.

Android backup

Like most Android apps, Manage Everything allows Android's built-in backup. If you have turned on backup in your device settings, Android may include the app's data in your device backup (for example, Google One backup) and restore it when you set up a new phone. This is managed by Android and your backup provider under your account and settings; the developer never receives or has access to these backups. You can turn backup off in your device settings.

Deleting your data

You can delete everything from within the app (Settings, then Delete everything), or remove all of it by uninstalling the app.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
